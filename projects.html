Truckload Margin & Cost Analysis

An interactive Power BI analysis using synthetic shipment data in the Truckload mode to analyze profitability, compare carrier costs, model margin targets, and prioritize lanes for follow-up. The goal is to give the Carrier Sales and Pricing teams a clear path from identifying a problem to deciding what to review.

INTERACTIVE REPORT SCROLL OR EXPAND TO EXPLORE
Where are costs potentially hurting our margins?Page 1 - Margin Performance Overview

Using weighted margin performance, teams get an overview on the weakest in the Truckload shipping mode.

How much money can we save, and what would it take?Page 2- Opportunity Model

Model lanes by target margin, and see what opportunity could be recovered, and what cost would be required across the lane to get there.

What action could we take, realistically?Page 3 - Priority Lane Action Plan

Ranks lanes by margin and opportunity. Low margin lanes and lanes with opportunity are ranked first.

Opportunity is simply the extra cost spent above the median price on that lane. It’s called Median Benchmark Opportunity in the report, as it treats the median as the benchmark.

Teams could use the median cost on the lane and the lane’s shipment volume as leverage to get the more expensive carriers down to a reasonable rate. This approach to opportunity creates a conservative yet feasible achievement for pricing and sales teams.

Details
Priority Rankings
  • High Priority: Underperforming lanes (below 11% margin) or low-margin lanes (below 15.5%) with significant opportunity ($900+).
  • Medium Priority: Lanes with moderate margin pressure (below 18%) with meaningful opportunity and above-benchmark loads ($350+, 5+ loads), or lanes below 19% margin with substantial opportunity ($750+).
  • Low Priority: Remaining lanes with opportunity.
  • No Action: Lanes with no opportunity.
Tools

Project was built in Power BI using DAX and Power Query, using entirely synthetic data to model transportation patterns to support the analysis.
